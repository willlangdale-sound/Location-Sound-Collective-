About

  • Nick is a studio-trained sound engineer with nearly 20 years experience working in audio. Starting out as live sound engineer, he has worked across multiple London venues and travelled the world aboard cruise ships as the head technician before settling back in London and discovering his passion for sound in film and television.
  • As a Boom Operator he has worked on high-end TV dramas and feature films, where he was lucky enough to be part of the sound teams behind the Emmy-nominated 3 Body Problem and the BAFTA-winning A Very British Scandal. These days as a Sound recordist and Quilified Drone Operator you’ll find Nick working on reality TV shows, documentaries, and short-form projects.
