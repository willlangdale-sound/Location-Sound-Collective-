Meet the experienced sound recordists and audio engineers at Location Sound Collective. Our team brings expertise in location sound, post-production, and sound design.

At the Location Sound Collective, we chase those moments — the resonance of a city at dawn, the hush of rain on glass, the echo that lingers long after the camera cuts.

We are a collective of field recordists, mixers, and designers united by one purpose: to capture truth through sound.
From feature films to documentaries and immersive experiences, our work shapes atmosphere, tension, and memory — turning silence into story.
