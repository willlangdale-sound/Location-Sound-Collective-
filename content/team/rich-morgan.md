Rich is a London based PSC sound recordist with many years of experience across TV, Feature Films, High-End TV Dramas, TVCs, Branded Content, Documentary, Corporate shoots, and BAFTA nominated short films. He delivers reliable, in sync, high quality audio in all circumstances and has a proven track record working confidently in demanding environments.

Rich has mixed everything from sports content to Tesco Christmas TVCs, and recently contributed to the Emmy award winning Masters of the Air, carrying out extensive vehicle recording work for the series.

He holds a UK passport, a full clean driving licence, is DBS checked, and is always willing to travel wherever needed. Friendly, hard working, and dependable, Rich brings professionalism and calm to every production he joins.
